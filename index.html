our story

Willow & Bean opened its doors in 2018 with a simple idea: a place where neighbours become friends over great coffee and honest food. We source our beans from small European roasters and bake our pastries every morning.

Our sunny corner café has become a second home for remote workers, families, and anyone seeking a calm moment. We're proud to be dog-friendly, community-driven, and fiercely independent.
